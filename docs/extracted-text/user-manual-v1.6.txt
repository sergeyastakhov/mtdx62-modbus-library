4.6 Common sources of interference and solutions
(1) Fan:Shakingfan(bladediametergreaterthan10cm),metalbladefanwithoutshaking,ceilingfan.
A fan with blades larger than 10cm that shakes its head can continuously output a manned state
fromthesensorthattriggersthemannedstatewithintherespiratorydetectionrange; Ametalfanblade
thatdoes notshakeits headwilltriggerormaintainthesensor's mannedstateoutputduringoperation;
Plastic fan blades that do not shake their heads can be filtered by sensors in areas outside of breath
detection. Settingappropriatetriggersensitivitycaneffectivelyavoidinterferencefromtriggeringsensor
outputs.
(2) Air conditioning: air deflectors for hanging and cabinet units, metal ornaments for air
conditioning blowing, thick curtains for air conditioning blowing, suspended ceiling vibrations
driven by ceiling mounted air conditioning, and louver vibrations for central air conditioning
blowing.

(3) Curtains:curtainsthatswaysignificantly,rollerblindswithaluminumalloylowerrails.
